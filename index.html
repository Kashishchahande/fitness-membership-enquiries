07

TURN STRATEGY INTO ACTION

Business Improvement Suggestions

01

Optimise Google Business Profile

Keep the location, opening hours, contact details, photos and services accurate and updated.

02

Offer an introductory visit

Give interested people a low-pressure way to see the facility and ask questions before joining.

03

Show real gym experiences

Post genuine trainer introductions, equipment tours and member stories only with permission.

04

Respond quickly to enquiries

Use WhatsApp Business quick replies for membership plans, timings, location and visit bookings.

05

Use referral campaigns

Encourage existing members to invite friends with a clear, realistic referral benefit.

06

Measure results every month

Track profile visits, messages, calls, enquiry sources, trial visits and memberships started.

THE OUTCOME

More visibility.
More conversations.
