7. Business Improvement Suggestions

  1. Improve Google Business Profile: Keep the address, timings, photos, facilities and contact information accurate.
  2. Collect genuine reviews: Invite members to share honest feedback after their experience.
  3. Post short videos: Share trainer tips, equipment demonstrations and class highlights.
  4. Offer a trial session: Give interested people a clear, low-pressure way to experience the gym.
  5. Use referral benefits: Consider a suitable reward when an existing member refers a new member.
  6. Make enquiries easy: Add a clear WhatsApp button, contact number, location and membership FAQ.
  7. Share flexible plans: Explain monthly, quarterly and student plans if offered by the centre.
  8. Measure digital results: Track profile visits, messages, calls, trial bookings and membership conversions.
